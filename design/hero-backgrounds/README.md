# Hero background concepts

This collection contains 177 background concepts for the frontend developer portfolio. All variant names, navigation labels, and design controls are in English.

Variants 101–140 explore minimalist abstract compositions spanning almost the full hero width. They use flat colors, fine lines, and geometric shapes without gradients.

Variants 141–150 develop Sparse Ticks with denser marks, pairs, clusters, staggered rows, and varied heights. Variants 151–160 develop Stepped Plateau with finer steps, layered contours, interruptions, and interwoven paths. Both series retain wide, subtle compositions without gradients.

Variants 161–168 continue the tick family with brackets, triplets, frames, ladders, varied intervals, points, and channels. Variants 169–177 continue the terrace family with cascades, courtyards, offsets, windows, switchbacks, notches, echoes, branches, and separated contours. These final additions also use flat colors and thin strokes without gradients.

Open [hero-background-preview.html](./hero-background-preview.html) in a browser. Use the previous and next buttons or the named picker to compare concepts. The preview follows the system's light or dark appearance.

[hero-background-variants.html](./hero-background-variants.html) is the editable fragment for inline design reviews. The browser preview is a standalone snapshot of that fragment, including the preview runtime, portrait, and fonts. Regenerate it with the `visualize` renderer after editing the fragment.

These concepts are design references. They are not included in the Angular application or its production build.
